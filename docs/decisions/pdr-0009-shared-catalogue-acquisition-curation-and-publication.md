# PDR-0009 — Collect, review, and publish shared recipes

- Status: Accepted
- Date: 2026-08-25
- Owners: Household product

## Decision and reason

The beta needs reviewed, useful content. Successful extraction is not publication,
and private household recipes must not leak into the shared catalogue.

Start with roughly `100–200` high-quality active recipes and meal options. This
is directional guidance, not a hard launch quota or permanent maximum. Before
external invitations, catalogue plus private imports must produce complete,
realistic weeks for the accepted synthetic households without shortages or
unreasonable repetition.

Review coverage across occasions, content kinds, effort, portable school/office
food, batches/leftovers, omnivore/vegetarian/fish options, proteins/cuisines,
equipment/windows and dependable low-burden fallbacks. No artificial quota applies
to every combination. Specialized needs may rely on private imports. Accept
readiness explicitly from coverage evidence, not a recipe count. Discovery
prioritizes practical structured filtering. Any later semantic index is
rebuildable, owns no recipe truth and cannot replace hard-suitability filtering.

Operators may bulk-import source links, initially TikTok, through source-neutral
candidate contracts. Processing several hundred links for the initial catalogue
is acceptable. Candidates remain private until publication; reject duplicates,
incomplete, unreliable or low-value extractions independently of household banks.

## Publication and source policy

Cillian is the sole MVP curator. Only that operator authority may publish,
correct, activate, retire or restore shared versions. Household membership or
importing grants no publication rights. Every publication explicitly creates or
activates an immutable version and is audited; neither extraction, household
approval nor review preparation publishes implicitly.

Review source attribution, ingredients, quantities, instructions, yield, scaling,
uncertainty, effort, equipment, windows, portability, leftovers, suitability,
shopping completeness, publication policy and useful coverage. Retain original
URL and reliably available creator/publisher identity.

Publish the normalized reviewed record, not original third-party video, audio,
images, screenshots, transcripts, captions or copied prose. Apply one documented
publication policy with one focused review before external beta. Attribution
alone is not a claim to resolve every legal issue. Retirement/takedown is audited
and preserves history. Per-recipe licensing, outreach queues and detailed rights
taxonomies are outside the MVP. Operator interfaces are optional clients of
catalogue commands, never independent authority.

## Private banks, versions and adoption

Households may import, author, approve and version private recipes. Their content
is not automatically contributed or projected globally. Editing catalogue content
creates a private fork linked to its exact source version.

Catalogue corrections create new immutable versions under stable identity.
Existing plans and household forks never change automatically. Future unforked
references use the latest active catalogue version; a household may preserve an
older version by forking it. Selective adoption into a fork is an explicit edit
creating a household version. Retirement affects future selection, not historical
plans or existing forks. Publication, corrections, lifecycle actions and adoption
remain auditable with reproducible version selection.

Delegated/multi-curator authority, public contribution or marketplaces,
retailer-funded content, autonomous publication, forced fork rebasing and original
media republication remain deferred.

# PDR-0012 — Choose a workable week using a repeatable policy

- Status: Accepted
- Date: 2026-08-26
- Owners: Household product

## Decision and reason

Evaluate a coherent week, not independent meal labels. Cooking, people, stock,
portions, routines and shopping affect one another. Recheck affected dependencies
after changes. An attractive preference score cannot excuse an infeasible plan.

Use these ordered selection layers:

1. Reject hard-constraint violations, unresolved managed coverage, missing
   compatible fallbacks, unavailable/unconfirmed stock, overdrawn or duplicate
   allocations, impossible equipment/location/preparation windows and excess
   admitted cooking capacity without an adult-approved exception.
2. Fit confirmed people and routines. Respect approved alternatives, strong
   avoids, exact-product/substitution policies and ordinary preferences. Prefer
   compatible shared meals/components over unnecessary separate work.
3. Reduce substantial cook events, hands-on effort, attention, coordination and
   cleanup. Use feasible hands-off starts, intentional leftovers and components.
   Elapsed time alone is not effort.
4. Respect accepted cadence. Keep dependable repeated foods; exclude/down-rank
   paused, avoided or cooldown-constrained meals appropriately. Honour favourites
   and make-again requests.
5. Where earlier priorities are comparable, reuse confirmed prepared food,
   planned output, components and ingredients already needed. Do not invent pantry
   stock or select a worse meal solely for overlap.
6. Use qualitative cuisine, protein, vegetable, method and history variety as
   a low-weight tie-breaker. It cannot override earlier priorities.
7. Break final ties with stable identifiers or another explicit stable order.
   Equivalent inputs and policy versions produce the same selection. Record them.

Never fill a gap with a weakly supported meal merely to appear complete. Expose
uncertainty/catalogue shortage or propose a flexible decide-on-the-day resolution.
Only the adult-visible draft/approval process makes it valid coverage; it creates
no recipe or shopping assumptions.

These priorities are MVP defaults. Adults influence them through ordinary
profiles, routines, fallbacks, cadence, equipment, capacity, exceptions and focused
requests, rather than numeric weighting controls. A configurable objective system
needs a new decision.

Explain actual selection factors using confirmed state, never post-hoc model
rationalization, private transcript text or an opaque aggregate score. Tests and
evals must cover attractive candidates rejected for excess work, bad cadence or
unavailable stock. The implementation formula may evolve while preserving policy.
Generic objectives, cost/nutrition optimization, retailer scoring, stochastic
recommendation and automatic seasonal/fleet tuning remain deferred.

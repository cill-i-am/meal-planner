# PDR-0008 — Handle visitors, temporary changes, and planning pauses

- Status: Accepted
- Date: 2026-08-26
- Owners: Household product

## Decision and reason

Temporary circumstances must not overwrite ordinary routines. Represent one-off
travel, absence, school/work changes, custody changes or cooking availability as
bounded exceptions for selected people and dates. Normal routine expansion
resumes afterward. Stable repeating patterns become versioned routines only after
adult confirmation. Manual conversational or visual input is sufficient.

A one-off visitor adds temporary demand for selected occasions, with a label,
approximate portions and necessary hard constraints. Do not create an account,
enduring profile, interview or household history by default. An adult may
explicitly promote a frequent visitor to a managed person.

An adult may suspend planning for selected people and dates, including a whole
household week. Suspension removes managed requirements and shopping responsibility.
It is distinct from not eating, eating elsewhere, deciding later or an unresolved
gap. Exclude suspended scope from incomplete coverage, failed return and unused-
plan metrics. A detailed reason is optional. Keep planning active with temporary
context when the household still wants help while away.

Applying suspension to a draft repairs coverage, cook events, output, allocations,
routine expansion and shopping, including dependencies outside the suspended
range. Explain the effects and expose unresolved dependencies. Applying it to an
approved plan creates a visible proposed revision, never a silent rewrite.
Acceptance releases future reservations and updates demand while preserving
manual and purchased shopping state and historical revisions. Early resumption
requires another explicit revision.

A whole-week suspension may use no plan or a suspended segment; neither requires
one fake skip per person and meal. External calendar/travel integration, visitor
accounts, complex custody management, automatic absence detection and
unconfirmed suspension remain deferred.
